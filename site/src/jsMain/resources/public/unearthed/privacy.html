Unearthed privacy policy

Unearthed: Dig & Discover, for Android. Last changed 20 November 2026.

This policy explains what the game Unearthed collects, why, who receives it and what you can do about it. In short: the game has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or any other personal information. It shows ads, and with your consent it sends anonymous information about how it is played. Crash reports are sent so we can fix bugs.

Who we are

Unearthed is made by C.B. Connect I.T., Hanegreefstraat 7, 3941 Eksel, Belgium, company number BE1004.248.918 ("we"). We are responsible for the information described here. Questions about privacy: christiano.bolla@cb-connect-it.com.

What leaves your phone

WhatSent toWhen and why

Never sent: your name, email address or any text you type; the optional play log (it stays on your phone unless you share it yourself). The game has no accounts of its own, no in-app purchases and no links that leave the app.

Legal basis

Where the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation applies, we rely on your consent for personalised ads and for analytics, and on our legitimate interest in showing non-personalised ads (which keep the game free) and in fixing crashes.

Who processes it

Google provides AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Google Play Games and Android backup, and processes this information for us. Read Google's privacy policy, how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Firebase's privacy information. Google may process data outside the European Economic Area, under the safeguards described in its policies.

How long it is kept

  • Analytics: the events of each dig for 2 months; what is linked to this copy of the game's random ID (user data) for 14 months. Then they are deleted.
  • Crash reports: 90 days.
  • Ad data: as described in Google's policies.
  • Play Games saved games and achievements: with your Play Games profile, until you delete them there or delete the profile.
  • On your phone: until you delete the game or reset your progress in Settings.

Your choices

  • Consent: in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the game asks at the first start, with Google's consent form, whether ads may be personalised and analytics may be sent. One choice covers both. Change it at any time in the game: Settings → Privacy → Your choice. Elsewhere no choice is asked: ads may be personalised and analytics is on.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads on most Android phones).
  • Play Games: sign out of Google Play Games to stop the cloud copy and achievements; manage or delete them in the Play Games app.
  • Your rights: under the GDPR you can ask to access or delete your data, object to its use, or withdraw consent. Because the game has no accounts, we cannot link analytics or crash data to you by name; write to christiano.bolla@cb-connect-it.com and we will help as far as we can. You can also complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be) or the authority where you live.

Age

Unearthed is for players aged 13 and up and is not directed at children. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game and want something removed, contact us.

Changes

When this policy changes we update it here and change the date at the top. Big changes are also mentioned in the game's update notes on Google Play.
