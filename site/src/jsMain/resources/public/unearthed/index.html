Unearthed

Dig & Discover · Treasures of the Earth

Somewhere under the rock is a T. rex skull, a sapphire, or a fossil fish nobody has seen for a hundred million years. Pick the right tool, dig carefully, and lift it out in one piece. Six dig sites with their own rock and weather, skeletons to rebuild bone by bone, a field journal to follow and a camp to make your own.
